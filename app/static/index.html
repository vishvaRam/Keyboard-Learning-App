What to look for

  • If every bar is the same colour, you are playing flat - that is the "no feelings" sound, visible.
  • Long bright bars with short dim ones between them = an accent, exactly what a phrase needs.
  • Bars that barely show up are ghost touches: the key did not reach the bottom.
  • A row of bars rising in brightness is a crescendo. Falling is a diminuendo.

Try this

  1. Play one note 10 times, each time a little louder. Watch the colour climb.
  2. Play a chord soft, then the same chord hard. Notice which notes are brighter.
  3. Play a melody you know and try to shape the last note down to a whisper.
  4. Press Freeze to hold the picture and study it, then Clear to start again.

Progression

Theory, plainly

Roman numerals (I, vi, IV...) number the chords of a key. In C major: I = C, ii = Dm, iii = Em, IV = F, V = G, vi = Am. Upper-case is major, lower-case is minor. That is why the same numbers work in every key.
